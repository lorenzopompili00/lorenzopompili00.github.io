I spent the first week of September in Corfu for the workshop
[*Binary compact objects on eccentric orbits and their gravitational-wave signatures*](https://sites.google.com/unimib.it/gweccentricity),
held at the Corfu Summer Institute.

On the first morning I joined Teagan Clarke, Gonzalo Morras and Sasha Husa on the opening panel,
*Detecting eccentricity: current evidence and future prospects*. We talked about where the current hints of
eccentricity in LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA events stand, and what it will take to turn them into confident detections.

It was great to see so much interest in eccentric binaries: the workshop brought together close to
ninety people working on theory, numerical relativity, waveform modeling, data analysis and astrophysics.

During the workshop week we submitted our latest paper on
**SEOBNRv6EPHM**, the first generic-orbit, spin-precessing model in the SEOBNR family. It follows
up on [SEOBNRv6EHM](/news/seobnrv6ehm/) by adding spin precession, and allows eccentric and precessing
effects to be analyzed together at a cost comparable to current quasi-circular analyses. Read the preprint:
[arXiv:2609.01568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01568).

Many thanks to the organizers, Isobel Romero-Shaw, Rossella Gamba, Davide Gerosa and Michalis Agathos,
for a very well-run and friendly week. Talking about eccentric orbits between swims at Corfu's beaches
was hard to beat!
